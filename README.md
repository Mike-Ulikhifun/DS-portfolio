## My Approach to Data Science

I follow a structured workflow to ensure analytical solutions
are reliable, interpretable, and aligned with the problem being solved.

1. **Problem Definition** — Establish objectives, constraints, and
   measurable success criteria.
2. **Data Acquisition** — Identify relevant sources and assess
   data quality, completeness, and reliability.
3. **Data Preparation** — Clean, validate, transform, and document
   the data.
4. **Exploratory Analysis** — Identify trends, relationships,
   anomalies, and potential biases.
5. **Model Development** — Establish baselines, engineer features,
   and select appropriate analytical methods.
6. **Evaluation** — Validate assumptions, select relevant metrics,
   and assess generalisation.
7. **Communication** — Translate findings into clear conclusions
   and actionable recommendations.
8. **Deployment & Monitoring** — Where appropriate, operationalise
   solutions and monitor their performance over time.
